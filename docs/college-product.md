# Product Specification & Vision — CAMPUS
*A simpler way to experience college.*
*Flagship Portfolio Product by Tetravate.*

---

## 1. Product Identity

- **Product Name:** CAMPUS
- **Subtitle:** A simpler way to experience college.
- **Creator:** Tetravate Concept Lab
- **Classification:** Flagship Education Operating Experience & Case Study
- **Primary Hypothesis:** Take the immense complexity of higher education enterprise resource planning (ERP) and make it feel as effortless, intuitive, and calm as a modern consumer productivity application.

---

## 2. Core Value Proposition

Traditional college management systems suffer from an information overload crisis:
- 15+ complex nested menus
- Hundreds of dense tables and administrative codes
- Disconnected data without personal context
- Clunky workflows that frustrate students, faculty, and leadership alike

CAMPUS solves this by enforcing:
```
Context ──► Prioritization ──► Personalization ──► Simplicity
```

Instead of forcing users to search for modules, the system dynamically answers:
> **“What do you need to know and do right now?”**

## 3. Personas & Operating Modes

### 👨‍🎓 Student: Aadhi Narayanan (3rd Year, Dept. of AI & Data Science)
- **Mental Model:** Needs immediate clarity on their day within seconds.
- **Key Workflows:**
  - Understand schedule, active class, and next room location immediately.
  - Track attendance and know how many classes can safely be missed.
  - Track upcoming deadlines (<48h) and mark tasks complete.
  - View exam seat numbers, instructions, and CGPA trends.

### 👩‍🏫 Faculty: Dr. Priya Raman (Associate Professor, Computer Science)
- **Mental Model:** Rapid-fire action during busy lecture schedules.
- **Key Workflows:**
  - Instant glance at today's classes and pending reviews.
  - Active lecture hero with 1-click `[ Take Attendance ]`.
  - 15-second roster workflow: 1-click `[ Mark All Present ]`, tap absentees, and hit save.

### 🏛️ Administrator: Dean of Academic Affairs
- **Mental Model:** Campus-wide operational pulse and exception handling.
- **Key Workflows:**
  - High-level campus vital signs (2,482 students, 148 faculty, 91.4% attendance).
  - Priority action queue: 3 timetable room conflicts, 7 pending approvals, 4 grade moderations.
  - Safety-gated controls preventing accidental destructive actions.

## 4. Feature Summary & Differentiators

| Feature | Legacy ERP Approach | CAMPUS Innovation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Home Dashboard** | 12 equal cards in a cluttered grid | Urgency-sorted linear sections: *Next*, *Attendance*, *Due Soon*, *Upcoming* |
| **Daily Schedule** | Static 5-day spreadsheet table | Chronological stream with real-time status and "Free for 90 Minutes" productivity insights |
| **Attendance** | Abstract percentage tables | Dynamic simulator: "How many classes can I miss?" with mathematical projections |
| **Assignments** | Database rows with raw timestamps | Prioritized task list with progress indicators and 1-click completion |
| **Exams** | Text list of dates | Chronological timeline with room numbers, assigned desk seats, and syllabus coverage |
| **Navigation** | 15+ bureaucratic dropdowns | Minimal Linear sidebar + `Ctrl + K` global command palette |
| **Mobile Experience** | Desktop site shrunk down | Fluid single-handed bottom navigation with 44px+ touch targets |
